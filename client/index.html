How to Use BuildCalc
1
Budget Builder
Critical-path project budget organized by construction sequence

The core of BuildCalc. Enter your budget for each line item in construction order — Materials, Labor, and Combined calculate automatically. The summary panel updates in real time so you always know your total.

  1. Set a project name, building type, and stories at the top.
  2. Enter Materials and Labor costs for each line item. Combined calculates automatically.
  3. Use the Status dropdown per row to track progress: Not Started → Bid Needed → In Progress → Complete.
  4. Toggle By Others on any row handled by a subcontractor — enter their name and the cost is tracked separately (excluded from your total but shown in the summary).
  5. Click the pencil icon on any row to add a note (e.g. "tile needs 6-week lead time").
  6. Fill in Overhead (taxes, loan, insurance), Soft Costs (permits, architect, geotech), and Other at the bottom of the table.
  7. Click Export PDF to print or save a clean budget sheet.
2
Blueprint Takeoff
Measure directly on uploaded PDFs and drawings

Upload a PDF or image of a drawing and measure linear footage, square footage, and counts directly on screen. Measurements push straight into the Project Estimator.

  1. Click Open (or drag & drop) to load a PDF or image drawing.
  2. Click Set Scale, click two known points on the drawing, then enter their real-world distance in feet.
  3. The Conditions panel (left) starts with 5 common conditions. Click + Library to search and add more from a categorized list, or + New for a fully custom one. Drag conditions to reorder them.
  4. Click a condition to select it, then draw directly on the drawing. Linear: click points, double-click to finish. Area: click corners, double-click to close. Count: single click per item.
  5. Already have a matching measurement elsewhere (e.g. Slab and Hardwood Floors cover the same area)? Click the copy icon next to a condition's Send to Estimator button to copy another condition's value straight in — no re-drawing needed.
  6. Use Hide Layer to click and hide a layer on the drawing, or Erase to click and delete a measurement.
  7. Use Push All to Estimator to assign CSI divisions and unit costs for every measured condition at once, then send to the Project Estimator. Conditions with nothing measured yet are skipped automatically.
  8. Use the Undo/Redo buttons (or Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Shift+Z) to step back or forward through any change — measurements, conditions, or reordering. Backspace removes the last point while drawing, Escape cancels.
3
Project Estimator
Detailed line-item estimating by CSI MasterFormat division

Build a detailed bottom-up estimate organized by construction division. Unit costs are national averages automatically adjusted for your region. Markup is applied on top to produce a final bid price.

  1. Set your project name and region in the top bar — this adjusts all unit costs.
  2. Click a CSI division in the left panel (e.g. 03 Concrete) to open it.
  3. Click + Add from Library to search and add items, or + Custom for a blank row.
  4. Enter quantities for each item — extended costs update instantly.
  5. Adjust Overhead, Profit, and Contingency % in the right panel, plus Mat. Sales Tax % and Permit Fees %.

Tip: every time you click Save, a version of the whole project is kept. Open the project dropdown at the top and choose Version History to review or restore an earlier save.
